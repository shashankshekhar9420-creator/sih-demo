# 🚀 Sahaj Artisan & Commerce — Demo Presentation Guide

This guide walks you through running the entire end-to-end demo on your laptop, showing how an Indian artisan creates a product catalog using **regional voice** and **local GPU AI**, publishes it to the marketplace, and handles real-time orders.

---

## ⚡ 30-Second Quick Start

Open **two terminal windows** (PowerShell):

### Terminal 1: Start Ollama LLM (Local GPU)
```powershell
ollama serve
```
*(If Ollama is already running in your background/system tray, this will say port 11434 is already in use — that means it is ready!)*

### Terminal 2: Start the Web Applications
```powershell
# 1. Reset / initialize databases (only needed once or to reset demo data)
npm run db:setup
npm run seed

# 2. Start both Artisan Studio & Commerce Marketplace
npm run dev
```

## 🎬 The 4-Act Live Presentation Script

### Act 1: Artisan Creation Wizard (Artisan Studio — `:4000`)
> **Talking Point:** *"Artisans often struggle with typing English, creating SEO listings, or photographing products on white backgrounds. Sahaj removes all of that friction through voice and local AI."*

1. Open **[http://localhost:4000/catalogs/new](http://localhost:4000/catalogs/new)**.
2. **Step 1 — Photos (Black Box #1)**:
   - Select 3 photos (e.g. from `demo-assets/images/` or any 3 craft photos).
   - Click **Save photos & continue**.
   - *Notice:* The system processes and normalizes the images onto clean backgrounds.
3. **Step 2 — Voice Answers in Hindi (Black Box #2)**:
   - Either record using your laptop microphone or upload the 3 sample audio files from `demo-assets/audio/`:
     - **Q1 (Product & Material):** *"यह प्राकृतिक नदी की मिट्टी से बना हस्तनिर्मित टेराकोटा पानी का घड़ा है।"*
     - **Q2 (Craft technique & look):** *"इसे पारंपरिक चाक पर गढ़ा गया है, धूप में सुखाकर भट्टी में पकाया गया है, और इस पर नक्काशीदार फूल बने हैं।"*
     - **Q3 (Use & tradition):** *"यह गर्मियों में पानी को प्राकृतिक रूप से ठंडा रखता है और त्योहारों पर उपयोग किया जाता है।"*
   - Click **Save audio & continue**.
4. **Step 3 — AI Catalog Generation on Local GPU (Black Box #3)**:
   - Select Category: **`Pottery & Terracotta`**.
   - Click **Generate my catalog**.
   - *Highlight to Judges:*
     - Watch your laptop's **NVIDIA RTX 3050 GPU** offload 33/33 layers of `mistral:7b-instruct-q4_K_M`.
     - In ~15–20 seconds, it produces a complete bilingual listing:
       - SEO English title & natural Devanagari Hindi title.
       - Storytelling craft descriptions in both languages.
       - Authentic bullet points highlighting techniques and materials.
       - Extracted technical specifics (material, firing, dimensions).
       - Marketplace search keywords.
5. **Step 4 — Transparent Pricing & Margin Calculator**:
   - Enter artisan making cost (e.g. `₹350`).
   - Show how the platform suggests fair pricing with margin breakdown (cost, packaging, marketplace cut, artisan net profit).
6. **Step 5 — Publish**:
   - Click **Publish to Commerce**.
   - You will see the confirmation badge: **"Published to Marketplace"**.

---

### Act 2: Buyer Experience (Commerce Marketplace — `:3000`)
> **Talking Point:** *"The moment the artisan taps 'Publish', their product is instantly live on the consumer marketplace with zero manual data entry."*

1. Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)**.
2. Point out your newly published product appearing at the top of the catalog.
3. Click on the product card:
   - Show the studio photos.
   - Show the bilingual language switch (English ⟷ हिन्दी).
   - Show the craft storytelling and technical specifications generated by the AI.
4. Click **Buy Now**:
   - Fill in buyer details (or use the pre-filled demo buyer).
   - Complete checkout.
   - *Notice:* The stock level immediately drops in real time!

---

### Act 3: Order Fulfillment (Seller Dashboard — `:3000/seller/dashboard`)
> **Talking Point:** *"The seller receives instant notification of orders and can manage fulfillment without complex logistics software."*

1. Go to **[http://localhost:3000/seller/login](http://localhost:3000/seller/login)**.
2. Sign in:
   - **Email:** `demo@sahaj-market.test`
   - **Password:** `demo123`
3. In the Seller Dashboard:
   - Point out the newly placed order in **PAID** status.
   - Change the order status to **DISPATCHED**, then **DELIVERED**.
   - Show the updated payout balance.

---

### Act 4: Artisan Business & Financial Health (`:4000/business`)
> **Talking Point:** *"Artisans don't just sell — they gain financial visibility with live profit tracking, demand trends, and payout schedules."*

1. Open **[http://localhost:4000/business](http://localhost:4000/business)**.
2. Show the two-way sync:
   - Live revenue and order counts matching the marketplace orders.
   - Inventory alerts for items low in stock.
   - Profitability analysis showing net earnings per craft item.
